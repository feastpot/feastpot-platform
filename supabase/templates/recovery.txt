Reset your Feastpot password
============================

Someone (hopefully you) asked to reset the password for your Feastpot account.

To set a new password, open the link below in your browser:

  https://feastpot.co.uk/auth/reset/start#{{ .ConfirmationURL }}

This link expires in 60 minutes. After that, visit the sign-in page and
request a new reset link.

If you did not request this, you can safely ignore this email and your
password will stay the same.
