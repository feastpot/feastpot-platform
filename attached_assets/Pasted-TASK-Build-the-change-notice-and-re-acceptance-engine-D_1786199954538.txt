TASK: Build the change notice and re-acceptance engine.

DEPENDS ON: LEGAL-500, LEGAL-502.

PRISMA SCHEMA

model LegalChangeNotice {
  id           String   @id @default(cuid())
  vendorId     String
  versionId    String
  sentAt       DateTime @default(now())
  channel      NoticeChannel
  deliveredAt  DateTime?
  openedAt     DateTime?
  acknowledgedAt DateTime?
  version      LegalDocumentVersion @relation(fields: [versionId], references: [id])
  @@index([vendorId, sentAt])
}

enum NoticeChannel { EMAIL DASHBOARD WHATSAPP }

FLOW ON PUBLISH OF A MATERIAL VERSION

1. Validation already enforced in LEGAL-500: effectiveFrom
   must be at least 15 days after publishedAt.

2. Enqueue a BullMQ job that, for every vendor with an
   active or suspended account:
   - Sends a Resend email containing the plain-language
     changeSummary, the effective date, a link to the new
     version, a link to a diff against the current version,
     and a clear statement of the right to terminate before
     the change takes effect without penalty.
   - Writes a LegalChangeNotice row with channel EMAIL.
   - Creates a dashboard notice row with channel DASHBOARD.

3. Dashboard banner appears immediately and persists until
   the vendor acknowledges or the effective date passes.
   Banner shows a countdown: "New terms take effect in N
   days. Review the changes or close your account before
   then." Do not make the banner dismissible without
   acknowledgement, but do not block the vendor from
   trading during the notice period either.

4. On the effective date, the new version becomes live.
   On the vendor's next login, present a re-acceptance
   click-wrap for the new version. Reuse the LEGAL-502
   component.

5. If the vendor continues to trade past the effective
   date without an explicit re-acceptance, record an
   acceptance with method DEEMED_CONTINUED_USE, capturing
   the same audit fields plus the specific action relied
   on (for example, accepted order ID and timestamp).
   Explicit re-acceptance is stronger evidence, so prompt
   for it, but record deemed acceptance honestly rather
   than leaving a gap.

6. Vendor declines during the notice window: route to a
   guided account closure that honours outstanding orders
   and catering bookings, and confirms the final payout
   date. Do not make declining harder than accepting.

EDITORIAL CHANGES
isMaterial false skips the 15-day wait and the notice
email but still creates a version record and appears in
version history. Require the admin justification from
LEGAL-500.

RATE SCHEDULE CHANGES
A change to Annex A is a change to the terms. Wire the
admin rate change form directly to this notice flow so a
rate cannot be changed without triggering it. This is the
dependency that makes source-based pricing (ORANGE-201)
lawful to introduce.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- Publishing a material version sends every active vendor
  an email and creates both notice rows.
- The banner countdown is accurate to the effective date.
- Changing a commission rate in admin cannot bypass the
  notice flow.
- Declining is no harder than accepting, verified by
  clicking through both paths.
