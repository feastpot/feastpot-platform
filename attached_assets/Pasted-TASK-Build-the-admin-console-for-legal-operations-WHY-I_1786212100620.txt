TASK: Build the admin console for legal operations.

WHY IT MATTERS
Every obligation implemented in this series is only worth
having if a founder can see, at a glance, whether it is
actually being met, and can produce evidence when asked.

SCREENS TO BUILD IN apps/admin

1. DOCUMENTS
   List all legal documents and versions with status
   (draft, published, live, superseded). Publish flow with
   the solicitor sign-off gate, changeSummary editor,
   isMaterial toggle with justification, effective date
   picker enforcing the 15-day rule, and a rendered diff
   against the current live version.

2. ACCEPTANCE COVERAGE
   The single most important view. Table of all vendors
   with their accepted version, acceptance date, method,
   and whether they are behind the current live version.
   Filter to "not on current version". Traffic-light
   summary at the top: percentage of active vendors on the
   current terms. This number should be 100 and any drop
   should be visible immediately.

3. NOTICE DELIVERY
   Per change notice: how many sent, delivered, opened,
   acknowledged. Flag bounced emails, since an undelivered
   notice is arguably not notice at all. Resend action.

4. ENFORCEMENT LOG
   Every restriction, suspension and termination with
   reason code, narrative, notice timestamp, whether the
   notice preceded the effect, and any appeal outcome.
   Highlight any action where notice followed effect
   without an urgent basis recorded, since that is the
   compliance failure mode to catch.

5. APPEALS QUEUE
   Open appeals with deadlines, reviewer assignment
   respecting the different-reviewer rule, and platform
   SLA adherence.

6. EVIDENCE EXPORT
   Generate a zip for a given vendor and date range
   containing: every version they accepted with content
   hash, acceptance audit records, notices sent, and
   enforcement actions with reasons. This is what you hand
   an insurer, a regulator or a solicitor if a dispute
   escalates. Build it before you need it.

ALERTS
  Any active vendor not on the current live version
  Any notice with a bounced delivery
  Any enforcement action with notice after effect and no
  urgent basis
  Any appeal approaching its deadline
  HMRC reporting deadline approaching

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- Acceptance coverage percentage is accurate and visible
  on the console landing page.
- Evidence export produces a complete, verifiable bundle.
- Publishing without solicitor sign-off is impossible from
  the UI, not merely discouraged.
