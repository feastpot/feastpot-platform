STEP 2 - DNS RECORDS (emit as a FOUNDER ACTION for the DNS registrar):
Recommend adding the domain in Resend as the SUBDOMAIN send.feastpot.co.uk (Resend's
default), NOT the apex, because: it keeps the apex SPF clean, isolates auth-sending
reputation, and gives clean DMARC alignment (Resend does strict DKIM alignment and
relaxed SPF alignment). Resend will generate the exact records; they follow this pattern
and MUST be copied verbatim from the Resend dashboard:
  - MX record on send.feastpot.co.uk (for bounce processing; Resend runs on AWS SES).
  - SPF: TXT on send.feastpot.co.uk = "v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all" (use the exact
    value Resend shows).
  - DKIM: TXT named resend._domainkey.send.feastpot.co.uk = the long public key Resend
    provides.
  - DMARC: TXT on _dmarc.feastpot.co.uk. Start at "v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:
    dmarc@feastpot.co.uk" to monitor, then ramp to p=quarantine and later p=reject once
    reports are clean. Explain why: DMARC ties SPF and DKIM together and tells receivers
    what to do on failure; starting at p=none avoids blocking your own legitimate mail
    while you confirm alignment.
  - If DNS is on Cloudflare, all these records must be DNS-only (grey cloud), because the
    orange-cloud proxy breaks DKIM verification.
Verification usually completes within 5 to 15 minutes but DNS can take up to 72 hours.
Instruct the founder to click Verify in Resend and confirm the domain shows "Verified".
