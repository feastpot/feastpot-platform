CONTEXT
As Prompt 25. UK English, no em dashes.

FIRST, INSPECT: read the Vendor model for businessName, slug, logoUrl, coverImageUrl and any description, story, cuisine, speciality, featured-dish and social-link fields. Note their current types, since several are likely comma-separated strings that should become arrays. Also read apps/vendor's "Share your kitchen" page to confirm the canonical link format that depends on Vendor.slug.

BUILD:

1. PROTECT THE SLUG. This is the highest-priority change on the page. Vendor.slug is used by the canonical share link and the downloadable QR codes in "Share your kitchen", and it drives vendor-referred order attribution. Editing it freely, as the page currently allows, silently kills every printed QR code, flyer and Instagram bio link the vendor has already distributed.
   - Render the slug read-only by default with a "Change" affordance.
   - On change, show a confirmation stating exactly what breaks: "Your QR codes and any links you have already shared will stop working. Anyone who scans an old code will see a page-not-found error."
   - Implement a redirect from the old slug to the new one and keep it permanently. Store previous slugs in a table so old links keep resolving. This is the correct fix and it makes the warning much softer.
   - Prompt the vendor to download fresh QR codes after changing.

2. CHIP INPUTS, NOT COMMA-SEPARATED TEXT. Replace Cuisines, Specialities and Featured dishes with chip inputs. Typing and pressing Enter or comma creates a chip; backspace on empty input removes the last; each chip has a remove control. Persist as string arrays, migrating existing comma-separated values by splitting and trimming. Enforce the existing limits (12 specialities, 6 featured dishes) by disabling further input and saying why, rather than silently truncating.

3. FEATURED DISHES COME FROM THE MENU. Do not let vendors type dish names that already exist as MenuItems. Replace with a picker over her live menu items, storing references rather than free text. If a referenced dish is later deleted or set to draft, drop it from featured automatically rather than showing a dead name to customers. If she has no live dishes yet, show an empty state pointing to Menu.

4. SOCIAL LINKS ACCEPT HANDLES. Change "Full https:// URLs only" to accept either a handle or a URL, and normalise on save. Entering "@mamanskitchen" or "mamanskitchen" in the Instagram field must store the correct full URL. Validate that the resulting URL is well formed; do not verify the account exists. Keep a website field that requires a full URL, since a bare domain is ambiguous.

5. EXPLAIN THE THREE OVERLAPPING FIELDS. Cuisines, Specialities and Featured dishes are not self-evidently different. Add one line of helper text each, phrased for a cook rather than a taxonomist: Cuisines is "the food traditions you cook in"; Specialities is "what you are known for"; Featured dishes is "the two or three dishes you want at the top of your page".

6. LIVE PREVIEW. Add a preview panel showing how the vendor's public page header will look with the current values: cover image, logo, business name, short description, cuisine chips. It updates as she types. This replaces a page that describes the customer view without ever showing it.

7. IMAGERY GUIDANCE. Beside logo and cover, state plainly why each matters and what happens without one, for example "Kitchens with a cover photo get noticeably more orders. Without one your page shows a plain colour block." Do not fabricate a statistic; if no data exists, phrase it qualitatively. Show the required dimensions as now, and validate on upload with a clear message rather than a silent failure.

8. COMPLETENESS. Add a short completeness indicator listing only what is missing and why it matters, linking to each gap. Do not show a percentage or a progress ring; a list of two remaining items is more actionable than "70%".

9. NAVIGATION LABEL. Fix the truncated "Bring your own custo..." item in the sidebar. Shorten the label so it fits, for example "Your own customers".

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- The slug is not editable without an explicit confirmation naming the QR-code consequence.
- Changing the slug creates a permanent redirect from the old value, and an old link still resolves after the change.
- No comma-separated free-text field remains on the page.
- Featured dishes can only be selected from the vendor's live menu items, and self-heal when a dish is removed.
- Entering "@handle" in a social field stores a valid full URL.
- The preview updates live and matches what the public vendor page actually renders.
- Field limits are enforced visibly, never by silent truncation.

EDGE CASES: a vendor with no menu items yet (featured dishes shows an empty state, not a broken picker); an extremely long business name in the preview (truncate with ellipsis, do not overlap, matching the header fix in Prompt 23); a slug collision with an existing vendor (reject with a clear message and suggest an alternative); migrating a vendor whose comma-separated speciality string contains more than 12 entries (keep the first 12, tell them which were dropped, do not discard silently).

STYLE: UK English. No em dashes. Sentence case. Do not add a second place to edit delivery or availability settings; keep the existing cross-reference note.
