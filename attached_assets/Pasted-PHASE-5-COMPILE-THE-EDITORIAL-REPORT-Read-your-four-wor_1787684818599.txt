PHASE 5: COMPILE THE EDITORIAL REPORT

Read your four working files under /audit/ and compile them into
a single editorial findings document.

FILE: /audit/FEASTPOT_AUDIT_<YYYY-MM-DD>.txt
Plain text. No markdown syntax, no tables, no emoji. Wrap lines
at 78 characters. UK English. No em dashes anywhere.

STRUCTURE (follow this exactly)

  FEASTPOT CODEBASE AND FUNCTIONAL AUDIT
  <date>

  Purpose
  -------
  What this audit covers, how it was conducted, and what makes
  it different from previous audits. State the commit SHA you
  audited.

  Executive Verdict
  ----------------
  Six to ten lines maximum. Is the platform safe to operate?
  Name the small number of things that matter most. Do not
  hedge. If the answer is "not yet", say so plainly and say
  what would change it.

  Priority 1 - Fix Before Any Real Transaction
  --------------------------------------------
  For each finding, in this format:

    <number>. <One line stating the problem as a fact>

    Why this matters:
      Plain language consequence. What actually goes wrong, for
      whom, and how badly.

    Evidence:
      file/path.ts:120-134 with a short quoted excerpt, or the
      command run and its real output, or the reproduction steps
      you performed.

    What to implement:
      Specific, actionable steps. Not "improve error handling".

  Priority 2 - Fix Within The First Weeks
  ---------------------------------------
  Same format.

  Priority 3 - Housekeeping And Technical Debt
  --------------------------------------------
  Same format, briefer.

  Confirmed Working
  -----------------
  Things you actively tested and found sound. This section is
  as important as the problems, because it tells me where NOT
  to spend effort. Only list things you actually exercised.

  Not Verified
  ------------
  Everything you could not test, and why. Be exhaustive and
  honest here. Missing credentials, unavailable services, live
  Stripe configuration, real bank settlement, anything requiring
  production access. A reader must be able to tell exactly where
  the audit's knowledge ends.

  Previous Findings Re-checked
  ----------------------------
  For each issue named in the earlier gaps audit and the vendor
  portal review, state one of: STILL PRESENT (with fresh
  evidence), FIXED (with evidence it now works), or COULD NOT
  REPRODUCE. Never carry a finding forward on faith.

  Audit Method And Limits
  -----------------------
  Commands run, apps exercised, accounts used, routes visited
  versus routes existing, tests executed. State what you did
  NOT do.

RANKING RULE
Order findings by risk, defined as consequence multiplied by
likelihood. Money loss, food safety and legal exposure outrank
everything. A cosmetic issue on a page nobody visits ranks
last, however easy it is to fix. Do not rank by ease of fixing.

TONE
Write for a founder who needs to make decisions, not for other
engineers. Plain language, no jargon where a normal word will
do, no softening. If something is bad, say it is bad. If you
are uncertain, say you are uncertain and say why.

FINAL CHECKS BEFORE YOU FINISH
  Every finding has real evidence with a file path or command
  output. Remove any finding you cannot evidence.
  No secret values appear anywhere in the file.
  No em dash characters anywhere in the file.
  The Not Verified section is complete and honest.
  You did not fix anything during the audit.

Then commit the report and the /audit/ evidence directory, and
give me a five-line summary in chat plus the count of Priority 1
findings.
