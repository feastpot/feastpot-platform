TASK: Implement the three-layer legal presentation.

WHY LAYERED
P2B requires terms in plain and intelligible language,
available at all stages including before contracting. A
single wall of clauses satisfies neither the regulation's
spirit nor a home cook's actual reading behaviour. Layer
the disclosure the way Deliveroo, Uber Eats and Just Eat
partner portals do, but proportionate to a small
marketplace.

LAYER 1: KEY TERMS SUMMARY
Source: Annex C of the terms. Ten plain-language bullets.
Component: packages/ui/src/KeyTermsSummary.tsx

MUST carry this label, visibly, not in small print:
"This plain language summary is a signpost only. It is not
the contract. Where this summary and the numbered terms
differ, the numbered terms apply."

Appears on:
  /become-a-vendor, below the commercials section
  The application form, above the submit button
  The acceptance screen, beside the full terms pane
  The vendor dashboard, under a Legal tab

LAYER 2: RATE CARD
Source: Annex A Rate Schedule, rendered from the same
data as LEGAL-505. A scannable table, not prose.

Appears on:
  /become-a-vendor (the page already states "12% on
  completed orders", so replace that loose claim with the
  generated Rate Card so the two can never diverge)
  The acceptance screen
  Vendor dashboard, Earnings and fees page

Show clearly which rates are live and which are marked
planned and not yet in force.

LAYER 3: FULL TERMS
/legal/vendor-terms, rendered from the database, with
version number, effective date, section anchors, version
history link, and a PDF download.

JOURNEY PLACEMENT MAP
  Marketing page      Layer 1 + Layer 2 + link to Layer 3
  Application form    Layer 1 + link to Layer 3
  Approval email      Link to Layer 3, PDF attached
  Acceptance screen   Layer 3 in pane, Layers 1 and 2 beside
  Onboarding call     Layer 1 as a talking-points sheet
  Dashboard Legal tab All three layers, always available

ACCESSIBILITY AND READABILITY
  Plain UK English, short numbered clauses
  WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, resizable text, keyboard
  navigation, screen-reader labels on the acceptance
  control, never colour alone to convey meaning
  Mobile-first: this is a PWA and most vendors will read
  on a phone. Test the acceptance flow one-handed.
  Downloadable and printable PDF of the accepted version

FOOTER CONSISTENCY DEFECT
The become-a-vendor page footer currently omits Catering,
Become a vendor, Vendor readiness and Trust and safety,
while the homepage footer includes them. Use one shared
Footer component everywhere so the Legal links are always
reachable.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- The signpost disclaimer is visible on every Layer 1
  render, not hidden in small print.
- The 12% figure appears nowhere as a hardcoded string.
- One footer component renders on every page of every app.
- The acceptance flow is usable one-handed on a 375px
  viewport.
