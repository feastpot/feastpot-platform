TASK: Let a vendor upload a menu photo and have Feastpot
build the draft menu.

WHY THIS MATTERS MOST
The target vendor keeps their menu in a WhatsApp broadcast or
an Instagram screenshot. Asking them to retype it into a
structured form is the largest single piece of work in
onboarding and a likely abandonment point. Removing it is
worth more than any other change here.

THE FLOW
  Vendor uploads one or more photos, screenshots or a PDF.
  Accept anything: camera capture, gallery, WhatsApp
  screenshot, a photo of a paper menu.
  Feastpot extracts candidate dishes: name, description if
  present, price if present.
  Vendor reviews a clean list, edits inline, removes anything
  wrong, adds anything missed.
  Everything is created as DRAFT, never published.

THE ABSOLUTE CONSTRAINT, DO NOT COMPROMISE
NEVER infer, guess or auto-populate allergen data from a
photo, a dish name or a description. Not from "peanut soup",
not from anything. Allergen declarations are a legal
obligation of the vendor as food business operator, and a
wrong inference is a safety incident.

Every extracted dish arrives with allergens EMPTY and cannot
publish until the vendor either declares allergens or ticks
free-from-all-14, per the existing gate. The allergen step is
the one part of menu creation the vendor must genuinely do.

MAKE THE ALLERGEN STEP FAST, NOT SKIPPABLE
This is where the conversion work belongs:
  present dishes in a single scrollable list, not one page
    per dish
  the 14 allergens as large tap targets
  "apply to all dishes" for a common base (many African and
    Caribbean dishes share stock, oil or seasoning bases)
  "copy allergens from a previous dish"
  a progress indicator: "12 of 18 dishes confirmed"
  save continuously so it can be done across several sittings
Frame it honestly and positively: "We have built your menu.
Now tell us what is in each dish so customers with allergies
can order safely."

PRICE AND PORTION HANDLING
If a price is unclear or absent, leave it blank and flag it
rather than guessing. Wrong prices are a commercial and
consumer-law problem. Same for portion sizes.

FALLBACKS
  Extraction fails or the photo is unreadable: say so
    plainly, offer manual entry, do not lose the upload.
  Vendor has no menu at all: offer a starter template by
    cuisine they can edit, prefilled with dish NAMES only and
    no allergens.
  Very large menus: cap the first pass at a sensible number
    and let them add more later. A vendor does not need 40
    dishes live to start trading.

WHAT THE VENDOR SEES
Present this as the platform doing work for them, because it
is. "Send us your menu however you have it. We will type it
up." That is a genuinely differentiated onboarding promise
and worth saying on /become-a-vendor too.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- A WhatsApp screenshot produces an editable draft menu.
- No dish is ever created with inferred allergens.
- No draft can publish without allergen confirmation.
- Bulk allergen application works across selected dishes.
- Extraction failure degrades to manual entry without losing
  the upload.
