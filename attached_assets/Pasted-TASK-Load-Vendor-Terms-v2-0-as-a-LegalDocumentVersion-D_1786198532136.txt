THREE DEFECTS IN THE LIVE TERMS TO FIX WHILE HERE

D1. Clause 3 of the live page contains a literal em dash:
    "Commission is deducted from your weekly payout - it is
    not charged separately." Also an en dash in
    "Monday-Sunday". Both must go (see RED-100 rule).

D2. Clause 7 of the live page requires vendors to accept or
    reject orders within 15 minutes or the order
    auto-cancels. This is unworkable for home cooks
    fulfilling scheduled orders and is not in v2.0. Either
    remove it, or replace with a response window that
    matches how scheduled ordering actually works (for
    example, respond by a stated time before the scheduled
    slot). Confirm which, then align code and terms. Do not
    leave a contractual rule the platform does not enforce
    or a platform rule the contract does not state.

D3. Clause 11 of the live page says nothing limits
    liability for "gross negligence or fraud". English law
    does not treat gross negligence as a distinct category
    in the way this implies, and the clause misses the
    statutory floor. v2.0 clause 17.1 uses the correct
    carve-out: death or personal injury caused by
    negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, and
    anything that cannot be limited by law.
