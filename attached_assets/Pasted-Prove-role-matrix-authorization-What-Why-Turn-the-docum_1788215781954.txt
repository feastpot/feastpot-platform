Prove role matrix authorization
What & Why
Turn the documented ownership and staff-role rules into real-token regression coverage. This protects admin, vendor, customer, and cross-vendor boundaries against future route-level authorization mistakes.

Done looks like
Vendor and customer tokens receive 403 on every admin endpoint.
Customer tokens and another vendor’s tokens receive 403 on vendor-only endpoints.
Vendor A cannot access Vendor B orders, payouts, menus, documents, disputes, or tax profile.
AAL1 staff is rejected from admin routes and AAL2 staff is allowed where their role permits.
Anonymous and authenticated incident-attribution scenarios are included.
Public menu availability, expired-document order blocking, and FHRS-suspended search exclusion are asserted.
Out of scope
Redesigning the authorization model or changing legitimate role permissions.
Adding test-only production endpoints.
Replacing the existing factory safety protections.
Steps
Extend the existing real-token test factory fixtures and isolated test namespace to cover the full staff, customer, vendor, and cross-vendor matrix.
Exercise representative endpoints in each protected resource family and assert both status codes and absence of cross-tenant data leakage.
Add the AAL1/AAL2 and public visibility scenarios, using the same verified sessions and database fixtures as production.
Run the matrix in CI with deterministic teardown and document any endpoints intentionally excluded because they are public or customer-owned.
