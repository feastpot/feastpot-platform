  C4 FeastPass     x  marketplace         x  fee waived
  C5 lapsed pass   x  marketplace         x  fee charged
For each assert the fee line: 5% capped at £2.99, or waived,
and that the VENDOR PAYOUT IS IDENTICAL in every case. A
FeastPass waiver must never reduce vendor earnings.

  vendor goes offline mid-checkout
  quantity exceeds remaining capacity
  discount code valid / expired / already used / not
    applicable to this vendor
  total price INCLUDING all mandatory fees is shown at the
