TASK: Rebuild the application into two short phases.

THE MEASURED PROBLEM
Vendor acquisition analytics: 24 page views, 24 calculator
uses (100%), 7 application starts (29.2%), 2 applications in
the admin queue. So 71% never start, and most who start do
not finish. Everyone who lands is engaged enough to use the
calculator, so the pitch works. The form is where they go.

PHASE 1: THE HOOK, TARGET UNDER 20 SECONDS
Fields, and nothing more:
  first name
  email
  mobile number
  postcode
On submit, create the lead record immediately. From this
moment the applicant is recoverable even if they never return
(see VO-05). Do not require account creation to reach this
point.

Above the form, state the commercial hook using LIVE RATES
from the rate engine, never hardcoded. The strongest true
line available is that vendors pay nothing on customers they
bring themselves. Lead with that, then the marketplace rates.
No upfront fee, no monthly fee, weekly payouts.

PHASE 2: CREATION, TARGET UNDER 2 MINUTES
  kitchen or business name
  cuisine type (multi-select, African and Caribbean
    sub-cuisines, plus other)
  menu upload (see VO-03), including the "I do not have a
    formatted menu, build it from my photo" option
  which occasions they want to cook for (reuse the existing
    occasion taxonomy)
Show a live preview of their emerging profile as they type.
Seeing their own storefront take shape is what creates the
incentive to finish.

MOVE OUT OF THE APPLICATION ENTIRELY
These currently appear too early and are a likely cause of
mid-form abandonment. Both current applications sit stuck at
FSA "Missing", one since 18 June:
  FSA registration number
  hygiene rating
  insurance certificate
  food safety certificate
  photo ID
  bank details
  tax information
All move to post-approval verification (VO-04, VO-05). None
is required to submit an application.

SAVE AND RESUME
Persist after every field, not on submit. If the applicant
leaves at any point, they can return via a magic link and
land exactly where they stopped. State this on screen so they
know it is safe to stop.

MOBILE FIRST
Most cooks will apply on a phone, likely from an Instagram
link. Test the whole flow at 375px. One question group per
screen, large tap targets, numeric keypad for the phone
field, postcode lookup rather than free text where possible.

ACCESSIBILITY
WCAG 2.2 AA. Labels not placeholders. Errors announced.
Fully keyboard navigable.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- Phase 1 completes in under 20 seconds on a phone.
- A lead record exists after Phase 1 regardless of what
  follows.
- No compliance field appears before approval.
- Abandoning mid-form and returning restores exact position.
- The commercial hook reads from the rate engine.
