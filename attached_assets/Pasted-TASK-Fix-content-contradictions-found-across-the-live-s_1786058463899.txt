TASK: Fix content contradictions found across the live site
and prevent recurrence with a single source of truth.

DEFECTS FOUND IN CRAWL

D1. The help page states "24/7 customer support" in one
    block and "Email support, Monday to Saturday" in
    another block on the same page. The footer partial
    varies between pages too. Pick one truthful answer and
    apply it everywhere.

D2. Two competing vendor signup routes. The help FAQ says
    email partners@feastpot.co.uk. The become-a-vendor page
    has an application form. This splits the funnel and
    confuses applicants. Make the form canonical. Keep the
    email as an accessibility fallback only, mentioned once.

D3. The help FAQ document list (hygiene certificate,
    insurance, ID, allergen training, Stripe onboarding)
    omits the FHRS minimum 3/5 that the Vendor Terms make a
    hard eligibility condition. An applicant could complete
    every listed step and still be rejected. Align the lists.

D4. Vendor Terms give a 7-day appeal window against a
    stated 5-business-day response time. An appeal can
    expire before its acknowledgement arrives. RED-102
    extends this to 14 days. Verify the fix landed here too.

D5. The Sunday family meal occasion page promises "reorder
    in a couple of taps and schedule the delivery day".
    Confirm scheduled reorder actually exists. If it does
    not, soften the copy until it does. This is also the
    natural hook for a future meal-prep subscription, so
    build it rather than removing the promise if feasible.

D6. The help page WhatsApp support link is a bare
    https://wa.me/ with no number. Either configure the
    number or remove the channel.

D7. Footer link sets differ between pages. Some pages show
    Catering, Become a vendor, Vendor readiness and Trust;
    others omit them. Use one footer component everywhere.
