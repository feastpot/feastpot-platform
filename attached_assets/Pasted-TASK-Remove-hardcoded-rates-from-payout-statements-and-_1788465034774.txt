TASK: Remove hardcoded rates from payout statements and
unify the statement model.

THE DEFECT
payouts.service.ts:1391-1395 computes a "saved versus"
comparison against a hardcoded flat 12%. With the new rates
this is actively wrong on a document a vendor uses for their
accounts.

REQUIRED

1. Remove the 12% literal. If a savings comparison is kept at
   all, compute it from the rates actually applied. Consider
   dropping it: with source-based rates, "saved versus a flat
   rate" is a marketing frame on a financial document. A
   statement showing the effective rate paid is cleaner.

2. ONE STATEMENT MODEL consumed by batch totals, order detail,
   CSV, PDF and the payout email. No format computes its own
   figures.

3. Every statement shows, per order: source (marketplace
   first, marketplace repeat, vendor referred), the rate
   applied, commission in pence. And in summary: gross sales,
   total commission, effective blended rate computed from the
   batch, refunds, chargebacks, adjustments, net payout.

4. Fix the detail inconsistency: batch creation includes
   delivered AND partially refunded orders (:849-860) while
   listPayoutOrders filters delivered only (:1118-1123).

5. Resolve the CSV contradiction: the comment at :315-323 and
   docs/OUTSTANDING.md describe fees and adjustments as
   placeholder zeros while :103-135 derives real values.
   Define the accounting meaning of every column and update
   the documentation.

6. Any genuinely unavailable value renders "not available",
   never 0.00.

WHAT VENDORS WILL SEE
Rates have fallen, so the first statements after the change
show higher net payouts. Say so plainly in the payout email:
name the new rates and what they mean for earnings.

TESTS
Build a batch for each and assert PDF, CSV and detail
reconcile: all-first-order (8%), all-repeat (5%),
all-referred (0%), mixed source with correct blended rate,
a batch spanning the effective date where earlier orders keep
old rates, a batch with a partial refund, a batch with a
catering booking at 10%, a batch netting to zero.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- No hardcoded rate in statement generation.
- Effective rate per order shown and correct.
- All formats reconcile to the penny.
- A batch spanning the effective date shows both rates.
