TASK: Fix defects found on the live site during this
review. Small fixes, disproportionate compliance value.

D1. EM DASH AND EN DASH IN LIVE VENDOR TERMS
Clause 3 of /legal/vendor-terms currently reads:
"Commission is deducted from your weekly payout - it is
not charged separately." with a literal em dash, and
"Monday-Sunday" with an en dash. These survived the
earlier sweep because the terms are hardcoded JSX rather
than shared copy. Fix in the v2.0 content, and confirm the
RED-100 ESLint rule and CI guard actually cover this file
path. If they did not catch this, the rule's file glob is
too narrow. Widen it.

D2. FOOTER INCONSISTENCY
The homepage footer includes Help and FAQ, Catering,
Become a vendor, Vendor readiness, Trust and safety plus
the five legal links. The become-a-vendor page footer
includes only Help and FAQ plus the legal links. Legal
links must be reachable from every page. Extract one
Footer component into packages/ui and use it everywhere.

D3. FIFTEEN-MINUTE ORDER ACCEPTANCE RULE
Live terms clause 7 requires vendors to accept or reject
within 15 minutes or the order auto-cancels with a full
refund. This suits a takeaway with staff on a tablet. It
does not suit a home cook fulfilling a scheduled Sunday
pot, which is Feastpot's actual model, and the site's own
copy emphasises scheduled ordering and lead times.
Decide and align: either remove the rule, or replace it
with a response deadline tied to the scheduled slot and
the vendor's stated lead time. Then make code and contract
match. A contractual rule the platform does not enforce is
as much of a problem as an enforced rule the contract does
not state.

D4. GROSS NEGLIGENCE CARVE-OUT
Live terms clause 11 ends: "Nothing in these terms limits
liability for gross negligence or fraud." English law does
not recognise gross negligence as a distinct category in
the way this implies, and the clause omits the statutory
floor that cannot be excluded. v2.0 clause 17.1 uses the
correct formulation: death or personal injury caused by
negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, and
anything else that cannot be limited by law. Ensure the
replacement lands and flag it specifically for the
solicitor review.

D5. VERSION LABEL
The terms page shows "Last updated: May 2026" with no
version number. After LEGAL-500, show both version and
effective date, and link to version history.

D6. APPEAL EMAIL ROUTING
Terms route appeals to compliance@feastpot.co.uk while
general support is support@feastpot.co.uk and privacy
queries go to privacy@feastpot.co.uk. Confirm all three
mailboxes exist, are monitored, and meet the stated 5
business day response. An unmonitored compliance mailbox
turns a compliant appeal process into a non-compliant one.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- Zero em dashes and en dashes across all legal pages,
  with the CI guard proven to cover them.
- One footer component on every page of every app.
- Order acceptance rule identical in code and contract.
- All three mailboxes verified as monitored.
