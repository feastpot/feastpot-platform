TASK: Fix the catering deposit calculation.

THE CONFIRMED BUG
apps/api/src/modules/catering-bookings/catering-bookings.service.ts:42-45

  depositPence = Math.max(5000, Math.ceil(totalPence * 0.25));
  balancePence = totalPence - depositPence;

A hardcoded £50 floor with no cap. Both quote paths accept
totals from 100 pence (:100-113 and :1128-1136). Any quote
between £1.00 and £49.99 produces a deposit larger than the
total and stores a NEGATIVE balance. A £1 quote yields a £50
deposit and a minus £49 balance. This also explains the
Total £0.00 / Deposit £50.00 screen I was shown.

DECIDE THE BUSINESS RULE FIRST, THEN IMPLEMENT
Two defensible options. Pick one, do not implement both:
  (a) Enforce a minimum catering quote total of £50 (or
      whatever floor makes commercial sense) and reject
      quotes below it with a clear vendor-facing message.
  (b) Cap the deposit at the total: deposit =
      min(total, max(floor, ceil(total * 0.25))).
Option (a) is cleaner commercially, since a £1 catering
booking is not a real booking. Confirm before implementing.

IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
1. ONE shared calculation function used by both quote paths.
   The duplication at :100-113 and :1128-1136 is how these
   drift apart.
2. Integer pence throughout. No floats anywhere.
3. Hard invariant, asserted in code: deposit + balance ===
   total, and balance >= 0. If the invariant fails, throw
   rather than persist. A quote that does not add up must
   never reach a customer.
4. Vendor terms clause 8.1 states 25%. Whatever floor or cap
   you apply must be reflected in the Rate Schedule and the
   terms, or the contract and the code disagree.
5. UI: mark required fields, explain why Save is disabled,
   and clamp quote expiry to the earlier of 7 days and 48
   hours before the event.
6. Allergen chips on this form: "cereals" should read
   "cereals containing gluten" and "nuts" should read "tree
   nuts". Ambiguity here is a safety issue, since peanuts is
   a separate regulated allergen with its own chip.

SCENARIO TESTS, BOUNDARY MATRIX
Test totals: 0, 1p, 99p, £1.00, £20.00, £49.99, £50.00,
£50.01, £100.00, £399.99, £1000.00, and a large event total.
For each assert: deposit correct, balance >= 0, invariant
holds, and either accepted or rejected per the chosen rule.
Also test: quote with zero line items; quote where a line
item quantity is zero; quote expiry clamping when the event
is 3 days away; both quote paths produce identical figures
for identical input.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- No input produces a negative balance.
- Both quote paths return identical results for identical
  input, enforced by a test.
- The invariant assertion throws rather than persisting.
- Terms and Rate Schedule match the implemented rule.
