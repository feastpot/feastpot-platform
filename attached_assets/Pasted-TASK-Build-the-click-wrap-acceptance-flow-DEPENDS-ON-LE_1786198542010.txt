TASK: Build the click-wrap acceptance flow.

DEPENDS ON: LEGAL-500, LEGAL-501.

LEGAL BASIS (state in code comments so it is not lost)
Electronic acceptance is valid in the UK under the
Electronic Communications Act 2000, the retained eIDAS
Regulation, and the Law Commission's 2019 statement on
electronic execution. Click-wrap is enforceable where the
vendor had reasonable notice of the terms and took a clear
affirmative action. No wet signature and no external
e-signature tool are needed for the core terms.

PLACEMENT IN THE JOURNEY
The live become-a-vendor page shows a four-step journey:
Apply, Quick review, Menu setup, Start receiving orders.
Acceptance belongs between step 2 and step 3. A vendor
cannot reach menu setup without accepting.

UI REQUIREMENTS (apps/vendor)

1. Full terms rendered in a scrollable pane, not a link
   the vendor is assumed to have read. Height at least
   400px on desktop, full-viewport sheet on mobile.

2. Track scroll position. Enable the checkbox only once
   the vendor has reached the end, or provide an explicit
   "I have read the full terms" affirmation if they use
   the download instead. Record scrolledToEnd honestly.
   Do not fake it to true.

3. Checkbox starts UNTICKED. Pre-ticked boxes are not
   valid consent. Label text exactly:
   "I have read and agree to the Feastpot Vendor Terms of
   Agreement version 2.0, including the Rate Schedule."

4. Alongside the checkbox: link to download the terms as
   PDF, link to the Key Terms Summary, link to the Rate
   Card.

5. The continue button is disabled until ticked. Keyboard
   accessible, screen-reader labelled, WCAG 2.2 AA
   contrast. This is a PWA, so it must work one-handed on
   a phone.

CAPTURE ON SUBMIT (all fields required)
  vendorId, versionId
  acceptedAt as UTC timestamp
  contentHash of the exact version displayed
  ipAddress
  userAgent
  acceptanceText, the exact label string shown
  scrolledToEnd
  method CLICKWRAP

Write the acceptance inside the same transaction that
activates the vendor account. If the acceptance write
fails, the account does not activate.

POST-ACCEPTANCE
Generate a PDF of the accepted version, watermarked with
the vendor name, version, hash and acceptance timestamp.
Email it via Resend as a durable record. Store it in
Supabase Storage and link it from the vendor dashboard.

RETENTION
Retain acceptance records and PDFs for the life of the
account plus at least 6 years after termination, aligned
to contractual limitation periods and HMRC record
retention. Add this to the data retention schedule in the
privacy policy.

DO NOT
Do not use a dark pattern to speed acceptance. Do not
bundle terms acceptance with marketing consent. Do not
allow acceptance on behalf of a vendor by an admin.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- A vendor cannot reach menu setup without accepting.
- All nine audit fields are populated on every acceptance.
- The PDF arrives by email and matches the stored hash.
- Checkbox is unticked on load and the flow is fully
  keyboard navigable.
