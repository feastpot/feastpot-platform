TASK 6 of 7: Catering funnel and occasion page bundles

STANDING RULES
1. CHECK FIRST. Read the existing /catering route and the occasion page template before writing anything. If an enquiry form already exists, migrate it rather than deleting it, and preserve any existing submissions.
2. Do not change the design system. Reuse existing form, card, progress and button components.
3. No new dependencies. Do not add a form library or state manager.
4. UK English, no em dashes. Typecheck, lint and tests pass before complete. Two identical failures means stop.

SCOPE: apps/web /catering and /occasions/[slug] only.

STEP 1: CATERING FUNNEL
Convert /catering into a six step funnel. One question per step, existing components only, step index in the URL as ?step=n so back and forward work and the form is resumable.
  Entry heading: "Planning food for 20 or more people?"
  Entry subtext: "Tell us what you need and we will help you find suitable vendors for your event."
  Step 1, "What are you planning?": Birthday party, Baby shower, Wedding, Church event, Office lunch, Funeral or memorial, Family gathering, Other.
  Step 2, "How many people are you feeding?": 10 to 20, 20 to 40, 40 to 80, 80 plus.
  Step 3, "What food style do you want?": Nigerian, Ghanaian, Jamaican, Caribbean, Mixed African and Caribbean, Vegetarian friendly, Not sure yet.
  Step 4, "Where should the food go?": postcode, event date, preferred time.
  Step 5, "What budget are you working with?": Under £100, £100 to £250, £250 to £500, £500 to £1,000, £1,000 plus.
  Step 6, contact details: name, email, phone, optional notes, hidden honeypot.
  Final action: "Request catering help".
Rules:
  - Validate per step. Do not let the user advance with an invalid postcode or a date in the past.
  - Show a progress indicator using the existing progress component.
  - Submit once to POST /v1/catering-enquiries from task 2. Disable the button while in flight and guard against double submission.
  - Confirmation screen: "Your feast request has been received. We will use your postcode, date, guest count and food preferences to help match you with suitable vendors." Include a secondary action back to /vendors.
  - Keyboard accessible: every option reachable by tab, visible focus, enter advances.

STEP 2: OCCASION PAGES
Each occasion page currently has a hero, an intro, a postcode search and FAQs. Add, using existing card components:
  a. Bundle cards by serving band, worded for the occasion:
       Small gathering, feeds 5 to 10
       Family party, feeds 10 to 20
       Big celebration, feeds 20 to 40
       Full event, 40 plus, action "Request catering help" linking to /catering with the occasion pre-selected via query string.
     Bands 1 to 3 link to /vendors with the occasion and serves filter applied after the postcode gate.
  b. A recommended dishes strip for the occasion drawn from the existing cuisine list, each linking to /vendors?cuisine=<slug>.
  c. A short trust strip reusing the homepage trust standard component.
  d. Keep the existing hero, intro paragraph and FAQs exactly as they are.

STEP 3: STRUCTURED DATA
Add FAQPage JSON-LD to each occasion page generated from the FAQs already rendered on that page. Do not add JSON-LD for content that is not visible on the page. Validate the output parses.

ACCEPTANCE TESTS
  - Complete the funnel end to end. Confirm one row is stored with every field mapped correctly, the internal alert and customer confirmation both send, and a second click does not create a duplicate.
  - Refresh mid funnel and confirm the step is preserved and prior answers are not lost, or that the user is returned to step 1 cleanly with a message rather than a blank screen.
  - Invalid postcode and past event date are both blocked with a clear inline message.
  - From an occasion page, the 40 plus card lands on /catering with the occasion pre-selected, and a serving band card lands on filtered results after postcode entry.
  - JSON-LD validates and matches the visible FAQs on two different occasion pages.
  - 375px viewport: the funnel is usable one handed with no horizontal scroll.
