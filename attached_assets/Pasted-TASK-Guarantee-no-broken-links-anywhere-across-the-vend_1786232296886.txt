TASK: Guarantee no broken links anywhere across the
vendor portal, customer PWA and admin panel.

CONTEXT
The vendor portal sidebar has 18 destinations. Three of
them currently fail (see VP-600). A previous review of
the public site found /trust linked from every footer
while returning 404. Broken links are recurring here, so
the deliverable is not a one-off fix, it is an automated
guard.

STEP 1: FULL CRAWL
Write a script at scripts/link-audit.ts that:
  Starts from the root of each app
  Follows every internal href and Link component target
  Records status code, redirect chain and final URL
  Follows links across apps (portal to public site and
    back, for example the Vendor Terms link on /terms
    pointing at feastpot.co.uk/legal/vendor-terms)
  Checks anchor targets exist, since the public terms
    page uses section anchors
  Reports external links separately, warning rather than
    failing on those

STEP 2: VERIFY THE FULL SIDEBAR
Explicitly assert all 18 vendor portal destinations
resolve with a 200 for a vendor with no data:
  Dashboard, Orders, Disputes, Menu, Availability,
  Analytics, Bring your customers, Catering bookings,
  Earnings and fees, Payouts, Compliance, Account status,
  Tax information, Terms and Notices, Profile, Team,
  Security, User Guide
Plus nested routes: /catering/new and any order or
dispute detail routes.

STEP 3: CHECK THE SPECIFIC LINKS SEEN ON SCREEN
  /terms: "Full Vendor Terms (Layer 3)" external link
  /terms: "Rate Schedule (Annex A)" external link
  /terms: inline "Vendor Terms of Agreement" link
  /tax-information: "Platform Operators Regulations 2023
    (SI 2023/817)" external link
  /tax-information: "gov.uk" UTR help link
  /tax-information: compliance@feastpot.co.uk mailto
  Sidebar "Contact support" button target
  "Go to Vendor Portal" button on error pages
Confirm each resolves and points where a vendor would
reasonably expect.

STEP 4: CI GUARD
Add the audit to CI. Fail the build on any internal link
returning 4xx or 5xx, or any anchor target that does not
exist. Warn on external link failures rather than failing,
since third party sites go down for reasons outside your
control.

STEP 5: MAILBOX VERIFICATION
Three addresses appear across the product:
support@, compliance@, privacy@. Confirm each exists, is
monitored, and can meet the 5 business day response
commitment stated in the terms. An unmonitored compliance
mailbox turns a compliant appeal process into a
non-compliant one.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- Audit reports zero broken internal links.
- All 18 sidebar routes return 200 for an empty vendor.
- CI fails when a link is deliberately broken in a test.
- All three mailboxes confirmed monitored.
