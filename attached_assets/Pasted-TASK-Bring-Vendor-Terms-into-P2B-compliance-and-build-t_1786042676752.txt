TASK: Bring Vendor Terms into P2B compliance and build the
notice infrastructure that the regulation requires.

CONTEXT
Feastpot is an online intermediation service under the UK
retained Platform to Business Regulation. Obligations apply
regardless of platform size. Small enterprises are exempt
only from the internal complaint handling system and the
requirement to name mediators. Terms that do not comply, and
changes made without proper notice, are void.

Current Vendor Terms gaps found on production:
  Commission percentage never stated (only on the marketing
  page), "subtotal" undefined, VAT treatment silent,
  chargeback liability absent, no notice period for changes,
  no termination notice period, no ranking disclosure, no
  data access description, payout holds have no maximum
  duration, and "Feastpot's decision on disputes is final"
  with a 7-day appeal window against a 5-business-day
  response commitment.

PART A: Rewrite the terms content
apps/customer/app/legal/vendor-terms/page.tsx

Add or amend these clauses:

  Section 3 Payouts, add:
    Commission is 12% of the food subtotal on marketplace
    orders. Food subtotal means the total price of food
    items only. It excludes delivery fees, customer service
    fees, tips and any promotional discounts.
    State whether commission is inclusive or exclusive of
    VAT and who accounts for it.
    Payout holds have a maximum review period of 14 days,
    after which funds are released or a written explanation
    is issued.

  New section: Chargebacks and fraud
    Define who bears card chargebacks, the evidence the
    vendor must supply, the response window, and the appeal
    route.

  New section: Changes to these terms
    Feastpot gives a minimum of 15 days notice on a durable
    medium (email and vendor dashboard) before any change to
    these terms takes effect. Vendors may terminate without
    penalty within the notice period.

  Section 9 Suspension, amend:
    Suspension or restriction is accompanied by a written
    statement of reasons issued at or before the restriction
    takes effect. Full termination requires 30 days notice
    plus a written statement of reasons.
    Replace "Feastpot's decision on disputes is final" with
    a two-stage internal review. Extend the appeal window to
    14 days to exceed the stated 5-business-day response time.

  New section: How vendors are ranked
    Disclose the main parameters determining position in
    search and browse results, and their relative importance.
    Disclose whether any paid placement exists and how it is
    labelled.

  New section: Your data
    Describe what data vendors can access about their own
    sales, customers and performance, in what format, and
    how to export it.

PART B: Terms versioning and notice infrastructure

PRISMA SCHEMA
model TermsVersion {
  id            String   @id @default(cuid())
  documentType  TermsDocumentType
  version       String
  contentHash   String
  summary       String
  publishedAt   DateTime
  effectiveAt   DateTime
  acceptances   TermsAcceptance[]
  notices       TermsNotice[]
  @@unique([documentType, version])
}

model TermsAcceptance {
  id              String   @id @default(cuid())
  vendorId        String
  termsVersionId  String
  acceptedAt      DateTime @default(now())
  ipAddress       String?
  vendor          Vendor       @relation(fields: [vendorId], references: [id])
  termsVersion    TermsVersion @relation(fields: [termsVersionId], references: [id])
  @@unique([vendorId, termsVersionId])
}

model TermsNotice {
  id              String   @id @default(cuid())
  vendorId        String
  termsVersionId  String
  sentAt          DateTime @default(now())
  channel         String
  deliveredAt     DateTime?
  @@index([vendorId, sentAt])
}

enum TermsDocumentType {
  VENDOR_TERMS
  CUSTOMER_TERMS
  PRIVACY
}

PART C: NestJS notice service
apps/api/src/terms/

When an admin publishes a new TermsVersion:
  effectiveAt must be at least 15 days after publishedAt.
  Reject the publish if it is not. This is a hard validation,
  not a warning.
  Enqueue a BullMQ job that sends every active vendor a
  Resend email containing the plain-language summary of what
  changed, the effective date, and their right to terminate
  within the notice period.
  Record a TermsNotice row per vendor per channel.
  Surface a persistent dashboard banner in apps/vendor until
  the vendor acknowledges.

PART D: Vendor dashboard
Add a Terms and notices page showing current version,
effective date, a change history with summaries, and the
acceptance record.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- A vendor reading only the legal terms can calculate their
  exact fee on a 100 pound order.
- Publishing a terms version with under 15 days notice is
  rejected by the API with a clear error.
- Every active vendor receives a notice email and sees the
  dashboard banner.
- Marketing page, help FAQ and legal terms state identical
  commercial numbers. Add a test that asserts this.
