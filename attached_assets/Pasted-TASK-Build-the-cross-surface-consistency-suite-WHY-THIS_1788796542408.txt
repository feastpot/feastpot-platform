TASK: Build the cross-surface consistency suite.

WHY THIS EXISTS
Every recurring defect in this product has been a
disagreement between surfaces: marketing said 12% while legal
terms said nothing; Settings said 12% while the engine said
8%; the payout PDF hardcoded 12%; the terms described rules
the code did not enforce. Individual app tests cannot catch
this class. Only a suite reading the same fact from every
surface can.

PART A: ONE FACT, FOUR SURFACES
Read from the customer site, vendor portal, admin console and
API, and assert all four agree:
  commission rates per segment
  the customer service fee percentage and cap
  FeastPass pricing
  catering deposit percentage and cancellation tiers
  payout cadence and the day payouts run
  vendor eligibility requirements (the document list)
  the current effective vendor terms version number
  support hours and contact addresses
  the 14 allergen names and their exact labels
Any disagreement fails the build and NAMES BOTH SOURCES.

PART B: ADMIN ACTION PROPAGATION
Perform in admin, assert in vendor and customer:
  approve a vendor -> vendor can sign in and set up; becomes
    discoverable for their postcode
  suspend a vendor -> disappears from customer search; vendor
    sees the reason and appeal route
  FHRS drops below 3 -> immediate suspension propagates to
    customer search
  publish a terms version -> vendor sees the notice banner
    with a correct countdown; re-acceptance prompt after the
    effective date
  change a commission rate -> vendor sees the notice; new
    orders use the new rate; earlier orders keep the old
  approve a menu item -> appears on the customer site
  reject a menu item -> vendor sees the reason; item never
    appears publicly
  refund an order -> customer sees it; vendor payout reduced;
    ledger records the pair
  resolve a dispute -> both parties see the outcome
  expire a vendor document -> renewal due, then suspended at
    day 7, then absent from customer search

PART C: THE REVERSE DIRECTION
  customer joins the coverage waitlist for an uncovered
    postcode -> appears in /coverage-waitlist. VERIFY THIS
    PIPELINE WORKS AT ALL; the waitlist is currently empty
    despite the form being live on the homepage.
  customer submits a catering enquiry -> appears in /catering
    with a correct SLA
  customer leaves a review -> appears in /reviews
  vendor uploads a document -> appears in /compliance
  vendor creates an item -> appears in /menu-moderation
  a failed notification -> appears in Dead letters
  a Stripe dispute -> appears in /chargebacks with its
    evidence deadline

PART D: DATA TRUTHFULNESS
  every percentage in admin has a denominator
  no metric renders green success against a zero denominator
  no raw UUID where a name exists
  test records distinguishable from real
  the audit log records an actor for every privileged action

CI
Required merge gate. This is the suite protecting against the
defect class that has recurred throughout this project.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- All nine facts agree across four surfaces.
- Every propagation completes and is asserted on the
  receiving surface.
- The coverage waitlist pipeline is proven working.
- A deliberately introduced disagreement fails the build and
  names both sources.
