## Published deposit clause requiring a new approved version

The current published clause states:

> On acceptance, the customer pays a deposit of 25% of the total (minimum GBP 50)
> through the Feastpot platform.

This conflates the minimum quote amount with the minimum deposit.
The calculation policy has a GBP 50 minimum quote, not a universal GBP 50
deposit floor.

Proposed replacement for legal review:

> **Deposit.** Catering quotes must total at least GBP 50. On acceptance, the
> customer pays the greater of 25% of the accepted quote total (rounded up to
> the next penny) or the vendor's stated minimum cash deposit through the
> Feastpot platform. The deposit is capped at the quote total and is
> part-payment of that total, not an additional fee.

Publish this only as a new approved Vendor Terms version through the existing
terms publishing flow. Obtain actual legal sign-off and determine the required
notice treatment before publishing. Do not copy old sign-off, rewrite a
published content hash, or change existing acceptance evidence.
