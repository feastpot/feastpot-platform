---
name: Mandatory fee disclosure
description: The user's legal requirement for the first customer-facing price display.
---

**Rule:** The total including the mandatory customer service fee must appear at the first point pricing appears, not be added late. Treat this as a legal requirement, not a presentation preference.

**Why:** The user explicitly stated this requirement for Feastpot customer pricing.

**How to apply:** When changing or verifying customer pricing, check the earliest price display, not just the final checkout summary. Calculation-only tests do not establish that the disclosure requirement is met.
