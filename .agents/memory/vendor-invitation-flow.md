---
name: Vendor invitation destination
description: The product expectation for approval invitations and their onboarding destination.
---

The vendor approval email's "Set my password & sign in" link must let the vendor choose a password, then continue to the real onboarding steps described in "What happens next".

**Why:** The user reported that the approval link did not lead to the promised setup process.

**How to apply:** Preserve the onboarding destination through confirmation and password setup, including resends. Keep generic invitation pages free of fictional demo-kitchen instructions and do not bypass onboarding readiness checks.
